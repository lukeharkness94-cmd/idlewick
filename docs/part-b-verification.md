## Verification boundaries

- The notification-only 60-minute controlled run used seed 7, with 12 minutes fishing, five cutting logs, eight cooking and 35 fighting goblins. The two end-state records matched for all item counts, coins, experience, kills and stats after restoring the sunken-chest roll. This checks the notification change independently of the campaign and balance changes.
- The 390px test checks the Home information units, Home controls, Home text scaling and panel contrast in both themes, plus horizontal fit in Shop. Several compact legacy controls still fall below the proposed global minimum, including the skill rail. Seventeen 48px rows cannot fit in a short desktop window alongside its header and Combat while keeping every row visible. The whole-game contrast and scaling audit remains open.
- Main remains the delivery branch. Steam Deck, native macOS and Windows behavior and Steam Cloud remain outside the verified scope.

## Measured sample

The scripted browser opening recorded 259 events in its first 60 in-game minutes, including 60 named loot summaries and four story dialogs. Two of those dialogs opened in the first five minutes. Routine levels opened zero dialogs. The development brief's earlier baseline recorded 272 events, 183 loot notes and nine dialogs, six in the first five minutes. The separate seeded engine comparison is the controlled reward-parity test.

The 390px Home audit measured 18px body text, 36px in the doubled-text test, no horizontal overflow, and no undersized Home buttons. Panel text contrast measured 12.5:1 in light and 10.9:1 in dark. These measurements cover the scoped surfaces described above.

The main regression suite has 122 checks. The standalone artwork and desktop-save checks passed. Browser walkthroughs passed for starter unlocks, exact quest routing, regional preparation, four offline durations, quiet routine rewards, exact level stops and the combined Home, Shop, restoration and save-string checks. The wider browser suite still has failures in older fixtures and layouts. Treat this as a development checkpoint and triage those failures before a release. The hours-three-to-five map records the remaining reward gap and the automated route from a fresh hero to Saltmere.
