# Skill identities and later progression — design proposal, 16 November 2026

Status: recommendations requested by the player, not implemented gameplay changes. Icon-led navigation and visual hunting stages are already implemented separately. New trap odds, recipes, specialisations and skill loops below need a coherent design/balance pass before implementation.

## Baseline findings

- Wooden-trap fieldwork currently belongs to Beastmastery, while Hunting primarily recommends quarry and starts ordinary combat. Their identities overlap.
- Companion expedition traps already have three tiers: Basic (40 coins, 1 hour, 40% common/10% rare), Iron (120 coins, 2 hours, 60%/30%) and Rune (boss-drop item, 4 hours, 80%/60%). Five misses guarantee the next attempt. Higher per-attempt odds plus a longer duration are not automatically better progression; compare expected captures/tracks per real hour including that guarantee, input costs and check-in frequency.
- Rune traps are currently rare boss drops; the higher trap tiers are weakly connected to crafting. Crafting/Smithing/Carpentry should provide meaningful equipment choices for this loop.
- Sailing already has five boat tiers opening different voyages: preserve this useful capability progression.
- Enchanting has five gem/rune ranks; later choices should alter equipment function or role, with clear costs and reversible decisions.
- Scholarship already has permanent studies and repeatable field notes. Evidence-driven projects should improve what the player can understand or attempt.
- New guilds currently provide finite practical quests, personal titles and seals. They need optional specialist projects/recipes to become a deeper endgame, while preserving the value and accessibility of ordinary progression.
- Workers already support finite jobs and stop wages at completion; extend that planning interface rather than creating a second automation menu.

## Shared model

Prepare → choose an approach → visible stages → useful result → new capability. Decisions happen before a run; saved plans and automation keep the game relaxed. Avoid mandatory repeated timing clicks, expiring windows, resource spoilage, an extra currency per activity and requiring every skill for every recipe. Existing tasks continue during navigation. Each skill should draw on two or three useful neighbours.

## Skill identities

| Skill | Proposed decision/loop and later capabilities | Useful neighbours |
|---|---|---|
| Fishing | Read water signs, select depth and bait, run a fishing plan. New equipment opens water layers, shoals and selective catches. | Crafting tackle; Herbalism bait; Cooking outputs. |
| Woodcutting | Inspect growth, plan a cut and choose useful parts to retain. Ordinary trees complete through reliable hits; later tools support bark, resin and precision timber. | Smithing axes; Carpentry timber; Herbalism resin. |
| Mining | Inspect a seam, choose an extraction path and sort the haul. Reliable baseline extraction; later tools open branching veins and selective mineral recovery. | Smithing picks/supports; Enchanting detection; Scholarship surveys. |
| Thieving | Observe a route, choose distraction/entry/escape, save the plan. Later jobs use social access, disguises and specialised targets. | Crafting disguises; Exploration leads; Herbalism preparation. |
| Farming | Arrange a bounded farm, rotate crops and choose feed/companion planting. Later progression opens new sustainable farm layouts and useful outputs. | Carpentry irrigation/pens; Herbalism soil treatments; Cooking feed/products. |
| Cooking | Choose preparation and method for an intended meal role. Better kitchens/techniques open meals for healing, voyages or specialist commissions. | Fishing/Farming ingredients; Herbalism seasoning; Carpentry equipment. |
| Smithing | Complete a commission through material, shape and temper decisions. Later work creates useful hardware and equipment roles. | Mining metals; Carpentry handles; Crafting fittings. |
| Crafting | Assemble modular designs: frame, cord, trigger or lining. Later recipes create precision, durability or specialist access. | Smithing mechanisms; Carpentry frames; Herbalism bait. |
| Scholarship | Combine actual field-guide evidence, compare a hypothesis and conduct a repeatable study. Unlock recipes, readable weaknesses and expedition opportunities. | Hunting observations; Mining samples; Exploration history. |
| Hunting | Survey signs, track behaviour, select a field trap/bait and recover wild materials. New equipment opens creature behaviours and habitats. Keep this identity distinct from fighting and companion care. | Crafting traps; Herbalism lures; Exploration habitats. |
| Exploration | Plan a route with supplies, choose an investigation and retain discoveries on a persistent map. Open shortcuts, rare habitats and story leads. | Carpentry field kit; Sailing coasts; Scholarship interpretation. |
| Herbalism | Identify useful properties and choose a blend for a situation. Later work supports lures, remedies and specialised preparation. | Farming herbs; Exploration rare plants; Cooking processing. |
| Enchanting | Arrange a small set of runes within clear constraints. Later choices alter an item's function: interrupt, protection, detection or specialist utility. | Smithing/Crafting bases; Mining gems; Scholarship patterns. |
| Carpentry | Assemble functional blueprints and choose a useful configuration. New construction opens workshops, farm equipment, trap frames and boats. | Woodcutting timber; Smithing joints; Crafting fabric/cord. |
| Sailing | Choose destination, cargo purpose, supplies and route. Boat upgrades open routes and cargo options; expeditions return useful discoveries or supplies. | Carpentry vessels; Exploration charts; Cooking provisions. |
| Beastmastery | Read each creature's preferences and choose care/training suited to its role. Deep bonds unlock abilities and specialisations with distinct uses. | Hunting tracking; Farming feed; Herbalism calming preparation. |
| Melee | Read the opponent, choose a weapon/stance and execute a saved opening plan. Later weapons change reach/rhythm/counters. | Smithing weapons; Enchanting effects; Scholarship enemy knowledge. |
| Defence | Choose guard, interrupt or counter priorities against readable telegraphs. Later progression opens defensive strategies rather than only mitigation. | Smithing shields; Herbalism protection; Beastmastery support. |
| Ranged | Choose distance, ammunition and target priority. Later bows/arrows support armour penetration, pinning and mobile opponents. | Crafting ammunition; Woodcutting bows; Hunting target knowledge. |
| Magic | Build a short automatic spell sequence around known enemy conditions. Later spells allow useful combinations and support roles. | Enchanting runes; Herbalism preparation; Scholarship discoveries. |
| Health | Prepare food, recovery and retreat policy for a planned encounter or expedition. Later progression improves endurance and recovery options. Keep it integrated with combat, not an extra screen. | Cooking meals; Herbalism remedies; Farming supply. |

## First complete slice

Prioritise Crafting/Smithing/Carpentry trap components → Hunting signs and bait → clear set/wait/check/recover feedback → useful materials or companion lead → Beastmastery care and training. Preserve existing companions and earned trap stock.

Suggested equipment roles: wooden snare for common fieldwork; reinforced trap for durability and larger quarry; padded live trap for companion expeditions; precision spring trap for selective targets; runic trap for late specialist behaviour. These are design roles, not approved final recipes/percentages. Existing boss-drop Rune traps can remain ready-made rewards if a crafting route is added.

After that, strengthen Exploration/Sailing as an expedition loop, then production commissions and evidence-driven Scholarship, then guild capstone projects. Guild rewards should express a skill's identity through optional signature work, while essential functionality remains reachable before level 99.

Acceptance: the player can explain the decision they made, observe their present stage, explain a later capability they are pursuing, and name another skill whose output helps. Human testing is still required; no engagement or retention results are claimed.
