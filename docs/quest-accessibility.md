# Quest accessibility correction — 18 November 2026

The Smithing 85 recommendation on “A map worth following” was caused by matching recipe ID suffixes: `smelt_bar_rune` was treated as a source for `rune`. The shared resolver now matches the completion event and exact primary output. Runes now resolve to Magic 1 (19 November follow-up); the separate rune-metal bar remains a Smithing recipe. The same resolver powers production objective navigation, fixing wildcard Smithing objectives that previously could select a smelting recipe and never advance the Smithing counter.

The audit covers all 133 currently defined quest entries, including 42 optional guild chapters, with real typed target references, levels and acyclic prerequisites. New quest offers wait until their direct gathering/production/survey skills and levels are available. A quest teaching a skill never requires already knowing that skill. Existing accepted quests retain their state and progress, and unmet prerequisites remain explained in Upcoming. A permanent survey completed before accepting Wren’s request also counts from its acquisition history, including after the chart is sold or a save is reloaded; it cannot strand the request behind a nonrepeatable task. The Wren survey quest now waits for its actual Magic, mining inputs and Exploration prerequisites instead of appearing as an opening assignment before those systems unlock. Guild recommendations identify their actual level-99 skill.

“Rats in the Granary” also asked an early player to bake potatoes before their farming introduction. Its clean-up meal now uses five shrimp, with matching dialogue and objective text. The two-objective structure, existing progress counters and rewards remain intact. No blanket XP acceleration, boss nerfs or save reset.

`questRecipeCandidates` / `questObjectiveTasks` / `questSkillNeeds` in meta.js are shared by recommendation/readiness and quest journal production routes. Delivery quests retain alternate shop/loot/worker sources; a production route is not treated as their mandatory skill gate. Boss danger advice remains distinct from entry requirements.

Validation: tests/quest-progression.cjs includes real rune production and the once-only survey, acceptance gates, accepted-save progress, introductory unlocks, granary reachability and the full catalogue. Regression (122), packaged journal journeys and cross-skill journeys are run alongside it. The old adventure-chain regression fixture now grants its actual Oak level and introductory crafting/farming permissions rather than injecting level-gated events into a fresh character. This is explicitly a synthetic fixture, not pacing evidence.

Reproduce the catalogue evidence with `node scripts/audit-quest-progression.cjs`; output is quest-accessibility-audit.json. The audit does not prove every supply chain is enjoyable, that rare drops arrive promptly or that all quests fit a desired time window. Human opening/returning-player tests are still needed.

The new whole-game UI directions are separate interactive concepts in /workspace/idlewick-concepts/whole-game-directions.html. They cover Home, activities, quests, combat, equipment, village, travel and shop; no concept's visual redesign has been applied to the live game. Their shared ethos is one clear next action, honest unlocks, work continuing through navigation, readable text and stable control placement. The directions are Hearthside journal (story-led), Wayfinder (task-led) and Village commons (world-led).

19 November: delivery objectives and production inputs also participate in new-offer readiness through player_feedback.js. The original accepted quest state is preserved. See experience-review-2026-11-19.md for the early Smithing and Magic inscription changes.
